A coach for focus and memory

Refrain

Meetings and pings break up your study time, and much of what you study fades by the next week. Refrain coaches both, from your own words.

  1. Focus for one session. Each time your attention slips, tap once and note what pulled you.
  2. Teach back the core idea in two or three sentences. The coach shows what's missing and turns your notes into one rule for next time.
  3. Recall it next time: the session opens with two questions to answer from memory.

Your plan starts at a length you can finish and grows from there. A meeting that cuts a session short never counts against you, and nothing resets to zero.

Your answers and notes are sent to Google's Gemini AI on Google Cloud only to write your coaching — Google doesn't use them to train its models. Your progress stays in this browser. Refrain is for adults 18 and over.
