Refrain

PRD approved · Oct 3, 2026 Laptop-first web app · English Canonical: devpost/prd.md

An AI coach that trains focus and memory in one study loop. Demo persona: a junior manager who studies after work and keeps getting pulled into meetings and urgent tasks.

From scope to product

“a four-question survey … the AI builds a roadmap”
→
One screen with validation (max minutes 5–120, never more than the daily minutes); 4–6 stages that never get shorter; sessions per day that fit the daily budget; a reason that quotes the user's own answers.
“tap ‘Distracted’ and optionally type a few words”
→
The tally rises the instant you tap, even with no note; a one-line field asks “What pulled you away?”; Enter saves, Esc or Skip closes; the countdown never pauses.
“if a meeting or urgent task pulls them away”
→
End early asks why. “I was pulled away” → jot where you stopped and your next step; the roadmap holds; the note greets you next session. “I lost focus” → ease back one stage.
“explain the core idea in two or three sentences”
→
You type the topic before the session so the coach can judge fairly; Get feedback stays disabled while empty; Skip still gives you a rule and a roadmap change.
“two questions … answer them from memory”
→
They open the next session as a warm-up; each answer is marked got it / partly / missed with a one-line answer; blanks count as missed; then they clear.
“up a stage, hold, or ease back — never back to zero”
→
Each stage allows one tap per 5 minutes (at least 2). Within it → up · more → hold · pulled away → hold · lost focus → ease back, never below stage 1. Taps never push you back.

Product decisions

Learner Your decisions

  • Project name Refrain — “to refrain” from a distraction, and “a refrain” you repeat until you know it by heart.
  • English interface — matches the English demo video and judges.
  • Audience is adults (18 and over) who want to retrain focus; the survey adapts the plan. Narrowed from “everyone” during 5-build to follow Google Cloud's generative AI terms.
  • Demo persona: a working adult like you — a junior manager whose focus is cut by meetings and urgent tasks.
  • The four survey questions: age, training goal, minutes per day, maximum minutes per session.
  • Memory training is a core goal alongside focus, to go deeper than Focusito.
  • Paid AI service (Gemini on Google Cloud), so users' study text and notes are not used to train Google's models.

Approved Proposals you approved after asking for research

  • Distraction notes → if-then rule; no streak resets.
  • Teach-back and next-session recall in the PoC; memory drills later; speed reading cut.
  • End early separates “pulled away” (hold + ready-to-resume note, Leroy & Glomb 2018) from “lost focus” (ease back).
  • Progression: one tap per 5 minutes allowed (at least 2); taps never push you back.
  • Laptop-first web app that also works on a phone.
  • The calm visual direction above.

Assumptions Approved with this PRD (“looks good”)

  • A “What will you study?” field before each session.
  • The survey is one screen; the goal field offers tap-to-fill examples.
  • Maximum minutes per session limited to 5–120.
  • A soft chime at the end of a session.
  • A page closed mid-session is discarded.
  • A data notice on the Welcome screen.

What we're building — and what we're not

Building now

  • Welcome with data notice; survey; AI roadmap with reason
  • Home: stages, active rule, pick-up card, topic field, demo-length switch, history
  • Warm-up with AI-checked recall
  • Dark focus session with one-tap logging and chime
  • End early: pulled away (where I stopped + next step) or lost focus
  • Teach-back with skip
  • AI debrief: got / missing / 2 questions / pattern / rule / roadmap
  • No-reset progression rules
  • Progress in this browser; reset everything
  • Friendly AI-failure state with retry

Deferred — tap for why

Accounts and sync

One device proves the loop; accounts add sign-in, storage, and privacy work.

Memory drills

Names & faces, phone numbers, memory palace — a second loop that would dilute the two-minute demo.

Prioritizing work tasks

Part of your story, but a separate problem with its own loop.

Spaced review beyond the next session

Needs a review calendar; the PoC proves recall at the next session.

Several good sessions before moving up

More realistic, but it would hide the roadmap change in the demo.

Tuning the tap allowance from real usage

Needs data from many sessions.

Keeping a phone screen awake

The laptop is the primary device for the PoC.

Vietnamese and other languages

English first for the hackathon.

Non-goals

Speed reading

Evidence shows a speed–comprehension trade-off.

AI summaries of the material

The user explains; the coach checks.

Blocking or automatic detection

Out of scope by design.

Calendar or chat integrations

The user says when they were pulled away.

Streaks, badges, leaderboards, social

The coach is kind and private, not competitive.

Medical or “brain improvement” claims

This is a study-habit coach.

Uploading notes or PDFs

The user brings their own material; the app trains how they use it.
